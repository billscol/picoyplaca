## Passage-Level Citability (sampled: Bogota FAQ, `plate_digit_day` model)

Content is server-rendered (`x-nextjs-prerender: 1`, full HTML present with no JS execution) — all FAQ text is visible to a crawler on first fetch. FAQ answers are generated by `web/lib/city-seo.ts` → `buildCitySeo().faqs`, and mirrored 1:1 into a `FAQPage` JSON-LD block plus visible `<h3>` question / paragraph answer pairs.

Word counts for the 7 Bogota FAQ answers: 19, 19, 19, 17, 11, 12, 29 words. **All are well under the 134–167 word optimal-citation range** — they read more like structured-data snippets than full extractable passages.

**Good example (self-contained, specific, quotable verbatim):**
> Q: "¿Qué días no puede circular un carro con placa terminada en 1 o 2 en Bogota?"
> A: "En Bogota, los vehículos con placa terminada en 1 o 2 no pueden circular los lunes, entre las 06:00–21:00."

This is exactly the kind of atomic, fully self-contained fact (city + rule + digit + day + hours, no pronouns needing prior context) that an LLM can lift directly as a citation with high confidence — this is a strength of the current content model.

**Weaker example (correct but thin, no reasoning/context an LLM could expand on):**
> Q: "¿El pico y placa aplica los festivos en Bogota?"
> A: "No. En Bogota el pico y placa se suspende los días festivos."

Only 12 words. Factually fine but offers no elaboration (e.g., "since [year]", "per [decree/article]", "except for X") that would let an LLM produce a richer answer while still citing this page as the source — right now a model would have to either quote it almost verbatim or supplement from elsewhere (diluting attribution).

**Best example in the set (closest to ideal length/self-containment):**
> Q: "¿Esta información aplica a motos y taxis?"
> A: "No necesariamente — estos datos cubren vehículos particulares. Motocicletas, taxis y vehículos de servicio público suelen tener horarios y excepciones distintos; confirma siempre con la fuente oficial de Bogota." (29 words)

This is the strongest passage: it scopes the claim, flags exceptions, and still stays self-contained. It's the template to replicate.

## Brand / Entity & Authority Signals

- **Organization JSON-LD**: confirmed present in every fetched page (`web/app/[locale]/layout.tsx`), rendered into raw HTML: `{"@type":"Organization","name":"Pico y Placa Global","url":...,"logo":...}`. Minimal but valid — no `sameAs`, `description`, or `founder` fields.
- **WebSite JSON-LD**: present alongside Organization.
- **FAQPage JSON-LD**: present per city page and matches visible on-page Q&A exactly (no drift between structured data and rendered text) — this is correct practice and reinforces trust that the structured data isn't spam/mismatched.
- **BreadcrumbList JSON-LD**: present (`Inicio > Ciudades > Bogota`).
- **Freshness/provenance signal**: confirmed rendered as plain visible text on the city page: *"Vigente desde 24 de julio de 2026 · Última verificación: 25 de julio de 2026"*, plus an outbound link labeled "Ver fuente oficial" pointing to `https://www.bogota.gov.co/pico-y-placa-test` (a real external government-domain citation). This is a genuinely strong trust signal — it gives an LLM (a) a specific verification date to reason about recency, and (b) a named primary source to cross-reference/co-cite, which is exactly what AI Overviews/Perplexity look for before trusting a secondary aggregator.
- **No `dateModified`/`datePublished` in any JSON-LD block** — the freshness date exists only as visible text, not machine-readable structured data. FAQPage, Organization and WebSite schemas have no date fields at all.
- **No Wikipedia / Reddit / YouTube / LinkedIn presence detected** — this is expected for a pre-launch/new site but is the single highest-correlation signal (YouTube ~0.737) for AI citation frequency per the brand-mention research. Cannot be fixed via on-page changes; flagged as a distribution gap, not a code fix.

## Structural Readability

- Heading order on the city page: `h1` ("Bogota, Colombia") → `h3` ("¿Cuándo tengo pico y placa?", the lookup widget title) → `h2` ("Preguntas frecuentes sobre Bogota") → seven `h3` FAQ questions. **The jump from h1 straight to h3 (skipping h2) before the FAQ section is a hierarchy violation** — the plate-lookup widget title should be an h2, or the FAQ block's h2 should come first.
- **The entire vehicle-category schedule section (the primary data table — "Particulares", "Motos", "Taxis", etc. and each category's specific days/hours) has no heading at all.** `PlateZoneSchedule`'s `CategoryCard` uses `CardTitle` (`web/components/ui/card.tsx`), which renders a `<div data-slot="card-title">`, not an `<h2>`/`<h3>`/`<h4>`. Translation strings like `labels.schedule_title` ("Zonas y horario de pico y placa") and `labels.exceptions_title` ("Vehículos exceptuados") exist in `messages/es.json` but are only used as small `<p>` micro-labels inside cards, never as actual headings. For an AI crawler building a document outline, this means the single richest data block on the page (per-category restriction data) is structurally invisible — it will be read as undifferentiated body text rather than a distinct, headable section.
- FAQ questions are correctly phrased as natural-language questions (good for question-based heading matching against user queries).
- Content is single-column, prerendered, no CSR-only gating — good for parseability once inside the page.

## Multi-Modal Content

- No video (YouTube embed/reference), no downloadable data (CSV/API doc linked from page), no comparison tables, no author bio/photo. This is the weakest dimension and also correlates with the weakest brand-signal category (YouTube 0.737 correlation). Not evaluated in depth beyond presence/absence since it's out of scope for a code-only fix, but flagged as the largest score drag.

## Technical Accessibility

- Fully SSR/prerendered (`x-nextjs-prerender: 1`, `x-nextjs-cache: HIT`) — confirmed via raw `curl` (no JS execution) returning complete FAQ text, JSON-LD, and headings. No SPA-shell/CSR risk for AI crawlers.
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` both resolve correctly; canonical tag present and correct (`http://localhost:3000/ciudades/bogota`); locale alternates via `hreflang` present in response headers (`es`, `en`, `x-default`).
- **"Today's restriction status" is communicated in machine-readable plain text, not just color/icon** — confirmed in `web/components/marketing/today-status-card.tsx` and `web/components/marketing/restriction-banner.tsx`: the status headline (`t("today.restricted")` / `t("today.free")`) renders inside a plain `<p>` tag; the icon (`CircleCheck`/`CircleAlert`) is purely decorative/supplementary. This is correct practice — an AI crawler or screen reader gets the answer from text alone.
- `plate-digit-lookup.tsx` is a `"use client"` interactive widget; its per-digit answer only materializes after a click (`useState`), so for a crawler with no JS/interaction, this specific widget's answer isn't in the initial HTML unless a `?placa=` query param is supplied. This is low-severity because the same information is already fully covered, in static server-rendered text, by the FAQ block and category schedule cards on the same page — the lookup widget is a UX convenience layered on top of already-citable static content, not the sole source of that data.

## Top 5 Highest-Impact Changes

1. **[High impact / Low effort] Add `llms.txt`.** Currently 404. Recommended location: `web/public/llms.txt` (static file, matches existing `public/manifest.ts`-style approach; no need for a dynamic route since city list changes infrequently and llms.txt doesn't need per-locale variants). Draft content below. Note: per general SEO knowledge, Google Search ignores `llms.txt` entirely — this is purely an LLM-crawler/citation signal (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, etc.), not an SEO play.

2. **[High impact / Medium effort] Give the vehicle-category schedule cards real heading elements.** Change `CardTitle` usage in `PlateZoneSchedule`/`CategoryCard` (`web/components/marketing/plate-zone-schedule.tsx`) to render as `<h3>` (or add an `as`/`asChild` prop to the shared `CardTitle` in `web/components/ui/card.tsx`), and promote the existing `labels.schedule_title` string into a real `<h2>` above the category grid. This turns the page's primary data table into a properly headed, extractable section instead of undifferentiated `<div>` text.

3. **[Medium impact / Low effort] Fix the heading hierarchy skip.** Either move the FAQ's `<h2>` above the plate-lookup widget's `<h3>`, or promote the lookup widget's title (currently `<h3>` "¿Cuándo tengo pico y placa?") to `<h2>` so the outline reads h1 → h2 → h3 without gaps.

4. **[Medium impact / Low effort] Lengthen the thinnest FAQ answers toward the 134–167 word band, or add 1–2 additional "expansion" sentences per answer** (legal basis, effective date, consequence of violation, or a scoped caveat like the moto/taxi example) using the existing `source_url` / `last_verified` data already available per city in `lib/pico-placa.ts`/city data — e.g. append "Esta restricción rige desde el {effective_from} según la fuente oficial de {city}." to the shorter answers. Keeps answers self-contained while moving them into the optimal citation-length range.

5. **[Medium impact / Low effort] Add `dateModified` (and `datePublished` if available) to the `FAQPage`, and consider a `WebPage`/`Article`-level JSON-LD with `dateModified` mirroring the visible "Última verificación" text**, plus enrich the root `Organization` JSON-LD with `sameAs` (once/if social profiles exist) and a `description` field. This makes the freshness signal machine-readable, not just visually rendered text, and strengthens entity disambiguation for LLMs building a knowledge-graph node for "Pico y Placa Global."
