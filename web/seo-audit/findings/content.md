# Content Quality — Pico y Placa Global

**Score: 61/100**

Scope: `lib/city-seo.ts` (template logic), `messages/es.json` / `messages/en.json` (`city_page`, `hero`, `faq` namespaces), and live-rendered city pages at `http://localhost:3000/ciudades/{bogota,barranquilla,bucaramanga,madrid,cali,cartagena,medellin}`. Audit-only, no files modified.

## What Works

- **Explicit trust signals per page.** Every city page renders `labels.last_verified` ("Última verificación: 25 de julio de 2026") next to `labels.effective_from` ("Vigente desde 24 de julio de 2026") plus an outbound `labels.source_link` ("Ver fuente oficial") to `rule.source_url`. This is a real, visible provenance signal most competitor pico-y-placa pages lack — confirmed present on bogota.html, barranquilla.html, bucaramanga.html, and madrid.html.
- **Self-contained, quotable FAQ answers.** The `plate_digit_day` strategy produces atomic factual statements, e.g. for Bogotá: *"En Bogota, los vehículos con placa terminada en 1 o 2 no pueden circular los lunes, entre las 06:00–21:00."* Each answer restates the city name and full context rather than relying on the question for antecedents — genuinely good AI-citation hygiene (no "it" / "this city" pronoun dependency).
- **FAQPage + BreadcrumbList JSON-LD** is emitted on every city page (`app/[locale]/(marketing)/ciudades/[slug]/page.tsx` lines 96–114), matching the visible FAQ accordion content 1:1 (no hidden-vs-shown mismatch, which Google's spam policies specifically flag).
- **Category-scope disambiguation FAQ.** `vehicle_scope.answer` — *"No necesariamente — estos datos cubren vehículos particulares. Motocicletas, taxis y vehículos de servicio público suelen tener horarios y excepciones distintos; confirma siempre con la fuente oficial de {city}."* — proactively answers a question a searcher for "pico y placa hoy" for their moto/taxi would have, and is appended to literally every city regardless of strategy (`buildCitySeo`, line 199).
- **Locale-aware formatting** (`Intl.ListFormat`, `Intl.NumberFormat`, `Intl.DateTimeFormat`) avoids robotic string concatenation for exceptions lists, currency, and dates — a small but real quality signal for both readers and AI extraction.
- **Barranquilla's "none" state isn't pure boilerplate.** Its `motos` category carries a genuinely unique, hand-entered note: *"Otros municipios del departamento del Atlantico si aplican esta restriccion desde diciembre de 2025."* This is exactly the kind of city-specific supplementary fact that should be leaned on harder (see Finding 1).

## Findings

### 1. [HIGH] Thin-content fallback state is live on at least one city, with only 2 FAQs and ~1 sentence of body copy — worse, it likely covers more cities than currently verifiable

`plateDigitDayStrategy.isActive()` (`lib/city-seo.ts:46-49`) treats the "particulares" category as inactive whenever `schedule.length === 0`. When that happens:
- `intro` collapses to one templated sentence: `plate_digit_day.intro_none` → *"En {city} actualmente no hay pico y placa vigente para vehículos particulares. {note}"*
- `faqs` collapses from the normal ~6 questions (3 digit-specific + hours + exceptions + holidays) down to **exactly 1** strategy FAQ (`faq_none_question`/`faq_none_answer`) plus the constant `vehicle_scope` FAQ — **2 FAQs total**.
- Title/description also switch to the shorter `meta.title_none` / `meta.description_none` variants.

**Confirmed live on Barranquilla** (`http://localhost:3000/ciudades/barranquilla`): rendered `<title>` is *"Pico y placa en Barranquilla: no hay restricción vigente"*, and the accordion contains only `¿Por qué no hay pico y placa en Barranquilla?` and `¿Esta información aplica a motos y taxis?` — nothing else. The full rendered HTML for this page is ~109KB vs. ~163KB for the active Bogotá page, and the removed ~50KB is almost entirely article content (5 fewer FAQs, no digit-lookup widget, no today-status card, since `heroCategory.schedule.length === 0` also suppresses the `PlateDigitLookup` and `TodayStatus` components per `page.tsx` lines 179 and 189).
- This is a **doorway-adjacent thin page**: for a plate_digit_day city with no active restriction, the entire page reduces to one sentence + two generic FAQs + the legal/contact/region boilerplate sections. That is unlikely to clear Google's helpful-content bar for a standalone indexed URL, especially at scale if more cities enter this state.
- **Could not fully verify prevalence**: `cali` and `cartagena` returned HTTP 500 during this audit (see Finding 5) and `medellin` timed out repeatedly, so I could not confirm their active/none status. Recommend the team run this same check (inspect `<title>` for `_none` vs `_active` suffix behavior, or check `findParticularesCategory(payload).schedule.length` directly against the API) across the **full city list** before shipping, not just the 4 cities I could reach.

**Recommendation:** For the `intro_none` state, add real supporting content instead of relying on 2 FAQs: (a) historical context — when/why the restriction was eliminated if known, (b) explicit note about other active categories in that city (motos, taxis, carga) if any have schedules — Barranquilla already has this data in `motos.note` but it's not surfaced in the FAQ set, only in a collapsed `<details>`, (c) a "nearby cities with active restrictions" module (the `CityRegionSection` "Otras ciudades con esta misma medida" already exists — confirm it also renders for `_none` cities, since it's structurally independent of `isActive()`).

### 2. [HIGH] Broken city pages return HTTP 500 / hang — a subset of the "content" is currently unavailable, not just thin

During this audit, direct requests to `http://localhost:3000/ciudades/cali` and `http://localhost:3000/ciudades/cartagena` returned **HTTP 500**, and `http://localhost:3000/ciudades/medellin` failed to respond within 15s across three attempts. `bogota`, `barranquilla`, `bucaramanga`, and `madrid` rendered correctly (200). This is adjacent to technical SEO but directly gates content quality: a 500'd page has zero E-E-A-T, zero words, and will be dropped from the index or flagged as a soft-404/server-error by Googlebot on next crawl. Given Medellín and Cali are two of the highest-search-volume "pico y placa" queries in Colombia, this is a high-value page currently non-functional. Recommend the dev/technical-SEO track investigate before this becomes a ranking/crawl-budget issue; flagging here because it directly undermines the content audit's ability to assess those specific pages.

### 3. [MEDIUM] Duplicate-content / doorway-page risk from template substitution is real but partially mitigated — quantify the unique-text ratio

For `plate_digit_day` cities, the *only* per-city variables are: city name, restricted digits, days, hours, exceptions list, holiday-suspension boolean, and (rarely) a hand-written `note`/`note_short`. Comparing Bogotá vs. Bucaramanga (both active, both `plate_digit_day`), the intro sentence structure, all 4 FAQ question templates, the vehicle-scope FAQ, section labels, and legal-section copy (for CO cities, `city_legal_section` renders the identical `legal.citation` — *"Art. 131-C.14, Ley 769 de 2002 (mod. art. 21, Ley 1383 de 2010)"* — verbatim on every Colombian city page) are **byte-identical apart from interpolated values**. Rough estimate: of the visible body content (intro + FAQ + legal + labels), well under half the running text is city-unique when a city is in the `_active` state, and it drops close to zero (just the digit/day/hour facts) when in the `_none` state.
- This is mitigated somewhat by: the JSON-LD/FAQ facts being genuinely different per city (not just cosmetically), the `source_url` differing per city, and `last_verified`/`effective_from` dates differing. Google's guidance treats programmatically-generated location pages as acceptable when each page has genuine topical substance and isn't just a template with swapped city names — this site is borderline-acceptable for active cities and weak for `_none` cities (see Finding 1).
- **Recommendation:** Increase the per-city unique-content ratio by surfacing already-available-but-underused data: `note`/`note_short`/`sub_rules` fields exist on `PlateCategory` (`lib/pico-placa.ts:53-58`) for categories beyond `particulares` (motos, taxis, transporte_carga, etc.) but are only shown in a collapsed `<details>` via `PlateCategoryNotice`/`RestrictionSchedule` — pull 1-2 of these into visible body copy or an additional FAQ per city where available, since they're already unique, sourced facts sitting unused.

### 4. [MEDIUM] Freshness signal is a static timestamp with no visible verification methodology or authorship

`last_verified` and `effective_from` are useful, but per Sept-2025 QRG expectations around trustworthiness, a bare date doesn't establish *who* verified it or *how*. The site-wide `faq` (homepage) namespace actually has better trust copy than the city pages themselves: *"Revisamos periódicamente las fuentes oficiales de cada ciudad. Los cambios pasan por revisión antes de publicarse — nunca se actualizan sin verificación."* This methodology statement exists but is **only on the homepage FAQ**, not linked from or echoed on city pages where the `last_verified` date actually appears. A searcher landing directly on `/ciudades/bogota` from Google (the far more likely entry point than the homepage) never sees this explanation.
- **Recommendation:** Either link the `last_verified` label to an "how we verify" anchor/page, or add a one-line tooltip/expandable using the same copy already written for the homepage FAQ. Low effort, directly reinforces Trustworthiness (weighted highest in E-E-A-T per this skill's model).
- Secondary, minor: `effective_from` dates observed during this audit cluster suspiciously close to the current date (Bogotá: "24 de julio de 2026", i.e., 2 days before the audit date) despite Bogotá's actual pico y placa dating to 1998. If this is seed/mock data, confirm it's replaced with real historical `effective_from` dates before launch — an incorrect "just changed 2 days ago" date on a decades-old restriction is a factual-accuracy issue (flagged explicitly as an AI-content risk marker in this skill's guidelines) and would be an easy inconsistency for a knowledgeable local reader to spot.

### 5. [MEDIUM] Footer disclaimer is present but low-prominence and singular (no author/organization identity)

`footer.disclaimer` — *"Información de referencia. No reemplaza la fuente oficial de cada ciudad."* (ES) / *"Reference information. Does not replace each city's official source."* (EN) — is accurate and appropriately humble, which is good. However:
- It renders as `text-xs text-muted-foreground` under a small logo lockup (`components/marketing/footer.tsx` lines 17-20) — the lowest-emphasis text style on the page, easy to miss.
- There is no visible "who runs this" / organization/company identity anywhere in the footer — no About page link, no company name beyond the product name "Pico y Placa", no legal entity, no editorial-team or data-team description. For a site whose entire value proposition is "trust our verified data enough to plan your driving day and pay for API access," the complete absence of an About/Team/Company page is a meaningful Expertise+Trust gap. `contact.title`/`contact.intro` on city pages ("Canales oficiales") link out to *each city's* official channels, which is good, but there's no equivalent "contact us" for the site operator itself.
- **Recommendation:** Add a brief About/Methodology page (who maintains this data, update cadence, correction process) and link it from the footer disclaimer, not just bury the explanation in the homepage FAQ.

### 6. [LOW] Readability is solid; a few long/jargon-dense sentences worth trimming

Spanish copy in `city_page` is generally short-sentence and direct (good for both human skimmability and LLM extraction). Two examples run long and stack multiple clauses:
- `plate_digit_day.faq_holidays_answer_active`: *"La fuente oficial no confirma una suspensión general en festivos para {city} — verifica antes de un festivo específico."* — fine, but this exact hedge-heavy phrasing repeats verbatim across every active city with unconfirmed holiday rules, which reads slightly robotic on a second city visited by the same user.
- `plate_digit_day.faq_exceptions_answer_none`: *"La fuente oficial no reporta excepciones generales para {city}; verifica caso por caso en la fuente oficial."* — repeats "fuente oficial" twice in one sentence.
- English (`en.json`) mirrors the Spanish structure faithfully and is equally readable; no major EN-specific issues found (not independently re-quoted here since it's a direct translation of the same templates).
- These aren't errors, just repeated hedge-phrase boilerplate that shows up identically across every city hitting that branch — reinforces the duplicate-content concern in Finding 3 more than it hurts standalone readability.

### 7. [LOW] Content depth vs. search intent — mostly covers the obvious questions, with one gap

For "pico y placa {city} hoy" intent, active-state pages cover: today's restricted digits (hero `TodayStatus` + intro), which days, what hours, which vehicles are exempt, whether it applies on holidays, and whether it applies to motos/taxis (vehicle_scope FAQ). That's a solid intent match.
Gap: no FAQ addresses **penalties/fines** in the FAQ set itself — `CityLegalSection` shows fine amount (`legal.fine_value`: "15 SMLDV") and consequence ("Inmovilización del vehículo") for CO cities only, as a separate visual card, not as an FAQ question/answer pair. "¿Cuánto es la multa por pico y placa en {city}?" is a very plausible high-intent query this template doesn't explicitly answer in FAQ/JSON-LD form, and it's entirely absent for non-Colombia cities (Madrid, and presumably any US cities) even though fines/consequences likely exist there too under a different legal basis.

## Recommendations Summary (priority order)

1. Fix the `cali`/`cartagena` 500s and the `medellin` timeout so those pages are actually assessable and indexable (High).
2. Audit every city in the live API for `_none`/thin-fallback state (not just the 4 reachable here), and enrich that state with real supporting content — nearby-city links, other-category notes, elimination history — rather than shipping a 2-FAQ page (High).
3. Surface existing but hidden `note`/`sub_rules` data as visible unique copy or extra FAQs to raise the per-city unique-content ratio (Medium).
4. Link the homepage's "how we verify data" FAQ answer from the city-page `last_verified` label; verify `effective_from` dates reflect real history, not seed-data recency (Medium).
5. Add an About/Methodology page and reference it from the footer disclaimer to establish organizational identity/expertise (Medium).
6. Add a fine/penalty FAQ pair to the template (localized per country's actual legal basis), and vary the repeated hedge-phrase boilerplate where feasible (Low).
